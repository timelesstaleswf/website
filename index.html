About Us

Wedding filmmakers who tell love stories in the language of cinema.

Honest, emotional and timeless. As a wedding videographer team working across Portugal, we take a discreet, empathetic approach, capturing the looks, the laughter and the little beats that make your day yours.

Based in Lisbon, we film across Portugal and abroad, bringing calm expertise and international experience to destination weddings. We specialise in destination weddings for couples travelling from the United States, the United Kingdom and Canada, and every call, plan and on-the-day direction happens in fluent English.

At Timeless Tales we pair emotional psychology with cinematic craft. We map your day's emotional arc and align lenses, rhythm, sound and colour to it. The result isn't just watched, it's felt again.

We don't film your day; we recreate the feeling of living it.
