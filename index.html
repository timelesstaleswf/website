The Founder

A wedding film should preserve emotion.

I'm Jason, founder of Timeless Tales Wedding Films. I believe a wedding film should do more than capture moments. It should preserve emotion.

With over 15 years of experience, I've filmed more than 300 weddings, including many alongside some of Portugal's most respected destination wedding filmmakers.

Today, I bring that experience into every film I create, crafting timeless stories that let couples relive one of the most meaningful days of their lives, exactly as it felt.
