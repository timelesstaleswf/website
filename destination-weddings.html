Why Portugal

Why couples choose Portugal.

Portugal offers something increasingly rare in destination weddings: variety within a small, easy-to-navigate country. In a single week, your guests can experience sun-drenched coastline, centuries-old palaces, working vineyards and a capital city with world-class food, all within a few hours' drive of each other.

Beyond the scenery, Portugal is genuinely practical to plan a wedding in. It's part of the EU, English is widely spoken in the wedding industry, flights from North America are direct and affordable, and costs are noticeably lower than comparable destinations like Italy or the South of France, without compromising on quality.

Planning Basics

Legal steps, timing and cost.

Legal vs. Symbolic Ceremonies

Many international couples choose a symbolic ceremony in Portugal and handle the legal paperwork separately at home - it's simpler, faster, and avoids extra bureaucracy and translated documents. A legal marriage in Portugal is also possible but needs more lead time. We recommend confirming current requirements with your venue or planner early on.

Best Time of Year
  • May – June: warm, green landscapes before peak summer heat
  • September – October: our top recommendation - warm days, softer light, Douro harvest season
  • July – August: peak season, hottest and busiest, book well ahead
  • Spring & winter: quieter and more affordable, still beautiful on the coast

On cost: Portugal generally remains more affordable than Italy, France or Spain for a comparable level of quality. See our filming packages for our own pricing.

How It Works

Planning from abroad, step by step.

  • Choose your season & region - timing and location shape everything else, so decide these first
  • Book your venue & key vendors - Portugal's best venues, photographers and filmmakers get booked 9–14 months out
  • Sort the paperwork - confirm legal vs. symbolic ceremony requirements early with your venue or a local planner
  • Plan guest travel - most couples host a long weekend; direct flights make this easy from North America and the UK
  • Arrive with time to spare - many couples land 3–5 days early to settle in before the big day
